Built for Salt Lake Valley

What matters for a court in Millcreek

The valley gets intense summer sun, cold winter inversions, and lots of freeze-thaw nights in between. Cheap paint chalks and fades fast up here, which is why we use acrylic court coatings with UV-stable color.

Older east-side homes often have existing slabs, tennis courts, or driveways that can be resurfaced instead of rebuilt. Newer west and south-valley lots tend to be flatter but can have soft or clay soils, so base prep is where we spend our time.

Tips before you build

  • If you have an old slab or tennis court, ask about resurfacing and adding pickleball lines before you pay for new concrete.
  • Rinse off road salt and de-icer that gets tracked onto the court in winter.
  • Check city setback and fence-height rules before you build near a property line.

Questions

Millcreek court FAQ

Does Ace Coatings work in Millcreek?

Yes. Millcreek is part of our Salt Lake Valley service area. We're based in Spanish Fork and build courts all over Utah, and estimates are free.

Can one court have pickleball and basketball?

Yes. Multi-use courts are one of the most popular things we build. We can lay out pickleball lines with a basketball key, and add a hoop, nets, and lights.

What does a court cost in Millcreek?

Price depends on size, whether you need new concrete, site prep, and add-ons like hoops, fences, and lights. Every yard is different, so we give free estimates based on your site instead of a one-size-fits-all number.

How long does a court take to build in Millcreek?

Resurfacing an existing slab usually takes a few days of good weather. A new court takes longer because fresh concrete needs to cure, often a few weeks, before it can be coated. We'll give you a real timeline at the estimate.

Do I need a permit for a backyard court in Millcreek?

It depends on the size of the slab, fences, and lights. Rules vary by city and HOA, so check with Millcreek's building or planning office and your HOA before you build. We can tell you what other homeowners usually need.
