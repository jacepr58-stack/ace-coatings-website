Built for Utah County

What matters for a court in Orem

Utah County sees hot, dry summers and real winters, with plenty of nights below freezing. Water that sits in a crack freezes, expands, and widens it, so drainage and crack repair matter more here than the color you pick.

Many newer neighborhoods sit on former farmland or on the benches above Utah Lake. Lower lots near the lake can have clay-heavy soil that holds water, and bench lots are often sloped. Both are workable when the base is graded and compacted right before any concrete goes in.

Tips before you build

  • Plan the slab with a slight slope (about 1%) so snowmelt runs off instead of pooling.
  • Ask your HOA early. Many newer Utah County subdivisions have rules on fencing, lights, and setbacks.
  • Face the court north and south when the lot allows, so nobody serves into the low sun.

Questions

Orem court FAQ

Does Ace Coatings work in Orem?

Yes. Orem is part of our Utah County service area. We're based in Spanish Fork and build courts all over Utah, and estimates are free.

How long does a court take to build in Orem?

Resurfacing an existing slab usually takes a few days of good weather. A new court takes longer because fresh concrete needs to cure, often a few weeks, before it can be coated. We'll give you a real timeline at the estimate.

Do I need a permit for a backyard court in Orem?

It depends on the size of the slab, fences, and lights. Rules vary by city and HOA, so check with Orem's building or planning office and your HOA before you build. We can tell you what other homeowners usually need.

Can you resurface my existing court or slab?

Usually, yes. If the concrete is solid and drains well, we repair cracks, prep the surface, and put down a new acrylic coating and fresh lines. If the slab has bigger problems, we'll tell you honestly.

Can one court have pickleball and basketball?

Yes. Multi-use courts are one of the most popular things we build. We can lay out pickleball lines with a basketball key, and add a hoop, nets, and lights.
