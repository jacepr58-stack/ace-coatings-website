Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Ace Coatings ("we", "us") collects through acecoatingsutah.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email info@acecoatingsutah.com or call 385-269-0060.

Information you give us

When you ask for an estimate, the form collects:

  • The kind of project you want (for example, a new court or resurfacing)
  • Your city or ZIP code
  • Your name and mobile number
  • Any court colors you designed or add-ons you picked on the site

When you send a design from our court designer, we also collect your email address, a picture of the court you designed (including any logo you upload), and a list of what you put on it. We keep that design so we can quote your project, and email it to you.

We also record the page you sent the form from and the time you sent it. If you call, text, or email us, we keep what you share in those messages.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your estimate and project by phone, text, or email
  • To prepare quotes, schedule work, and provide our services
  • To keep records of our customers and projects
  • To understand how people use our site and how well our ads work

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may call or text you about your estimate or project. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time, or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our customer management (CRM) software, website hosting, email delivery, and phone and texting providers. They may use it only to provide services to us. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Our site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, and Google Ads and the Meta (Facebook) pixel to measure and improve our advertising. These tools use cookies and similar technology to collect information such as pages visited, the device and browser you use, and how you reached the site. Google and Meta may use this information as described in their own privacy policies.

You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, change ad personalization at Google Ad Settings, and manage Meta ads in your Facebook ad preferences. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it to respond to you, provide our services, and keep business and tax records, and then delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you by emailing info@acecoatingsutah.com. We may need to keep some records when the law requires it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No website or system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee security.

Children

Our site is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
